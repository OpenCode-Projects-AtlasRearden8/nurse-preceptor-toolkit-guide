# Readiness Summary (One Page, End of Precepting)

**Education only — this guide is never medical advice.** Companion to [chapter 9](../docs/09-handoff-readiness-summary.html). The coaching-side summary that lives *beside* your facility's official competency paperwork — it validates nothing and replaces nothing. Fill it from the [precept hours log](../worksheets/precept-hours-log.html) and weekly plans; the Thursday conversation reads this draft together, their margins first.

## Handoff paragraph for the educator (two minutes, real material)

```
Here's [initials]'s [N] weeks in one view: independent at [task family] since
week [n]; autonomy rungs per task family attached; one watch-behavior named
with the coaching plan tested; one protected habit; hours-vs-readiness both
stated above. Ask me anything.
```

## The farewell (Friday — chapter 9's three parts)

```

My open door sentence: "Tell me in six months how month three went — genuinely."
The last word: theirs. Received without editing. [ ]
```

**Reminder:** two halves, two separate facts, their margins first, their words last — and the official paperwork stays exactly as official as it was.
