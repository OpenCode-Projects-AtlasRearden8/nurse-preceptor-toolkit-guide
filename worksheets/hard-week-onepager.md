# Hard Week One-Pager

**Education only — this guide is never medical advice.** Companion to [chapter 8](../docs/08-surviving-the-difficult-week.html). Keep two copies in your locker: one for the week your orientee is having, one for the week you are having. Three minutes to use mid-crisis — the plan was the work.

## First, name the shape (chapter 8's five — pick one)

[ ] The stall — plateau, flat for days        [ ] The tears — crying in the med room
[ ] The freeze repeats — freezing multiple days   [ ] The drain — I am the empty one
[ ] The mismatch — weeks of values/engagement friction despite everything

## Immediate moves (shape-by-shape, from chapter 8)

```
Stall:  → shrink one rung for 2-3 days · one skill once · bank the win
        → start from their original reason-for-this-unit (day-one question)
Tears:  → change the physics (move rooms) → one permission sentence → silence
        → one truthful anchor WITH the log as proof → adjust ONE thing, not the world
Freeze: → name it stress-not-capacity → ladder-down drill (shrink the question by half)
        → two-options offer → loop educator in about what is actually frozen
Drain:  → trade one rung for rest → guard two real things today → say the gauge out loud
        → ask for real relief where your unit offers it — asking is planning
Mismatch: → two weeks of adjustment attempts → educator, evidence from my log, on time
          → let the proper channels own the outcome — this is not my solo verdict
```
