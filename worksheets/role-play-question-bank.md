**Education only — this guide is never medical advice.** Companion to [chapter 4](../docs/04-growing-critical-thinking.html). ## The three anchors (always in stock)

- [ ] "What would you do if I weren't here?"
- [ ] "What are you watching for next — and what would change your mind?"
- [ ] "What's the part you're least sure about?"
