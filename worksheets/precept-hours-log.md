# Precept Hours Log

**Education only — this guide is never medical advice.** Companion to [chapter 7](../docs/07-documenting-precepting-hours.html). Your memory aid and receipt-book, one row per shift. **Your facility's official timesheet and competency forms remain the record of record** — this log feeds them; it never replaces them. Initials only; no patient specifics anywhere on this sheet.

```

Unit orientation length (weeks): ______   Expected weekly hours: ______
Educator: ______________   Facility timesheet due by: ______________
```
